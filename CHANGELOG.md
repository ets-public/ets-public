# Changelog

Every version, newest first. Training, blood tests and health need a licence; everything else is in the free edition.

## 1.0.0 · First public release

- Injections: schedules, cycles (e.g. 6 or 12 weeks), reminders, dose logging with syringe draw, site rotation, vial stock and estimated levels
- Labs: results over time, RoidSafe PDF import, doctor report PDF
- Training: templates, rest timer, records with **Heaviest** and **Est. max**, progressive overload suggestions, Strong import
- Cardio: GPS runs, walks and hikes with route maps, splits and elevation; treadmill runs
- Health: Health Connect data, live heart rate from Bluetooth bands, readiness score, weekly report, body profile
- Backups to your own Google Drive, app lock
