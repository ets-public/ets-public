# Changelog

Every version, newest first. Training, blood tests and health need a licence; everything else is in the free edition.

## 1.0.3 · 30 Sept 2026

### Free

- "Show older injections" in History works in the free edition

### Licence

- Blood tests: plan your next test for the right morning before an injection, with reminders, and set your own alert range for any marker
- Training: programmes that run your templates week by week, with planned progression and deload weeks
- Body: tape measurements with trends, and progress photos that stay on your phone
- Blood tests: "Free Testosterone Index" no longer replaces your free testosterone result; add a time to hand-entered results
- Doctor report lists every blood test date in the period, and results in different units separately
- Records: light high-rep sets no longer count as a new estimated max; dumbbells always suggest 2 kg jumps
- Workouts: unticked sets you never typed into aren't offered as done; undo for "− Last set"; a weight is needed before ticking
- Health: sleep is no longer counted twice when two apps record the same night; blood pressure shows both numbers
- Runs paused from the notification stay in step with the app
- Show and copy your licence key in App settings

### Both editions

- New Stock tab: see every vial you have (open and sealed) with its size and strength, how much you have in mg, and how long it lasts at your doses. Opening a new vial takes it off your sealed count, and you're warned when you're on your last vial
- Stock: reorder reminder based on your delivery time, cost per week and month, and expiry dates and batch numbers on vials (the soonest to expire is opened first)
- New daily check-in: rate your energy, mood, libido and sleep and note injection-site reactions; see them on the levels chart and in the doctor report
- Stock and planning reminders, and a check-in reminder, in App settings > Notifications
- Security: a crafted backup file can no longer run code in the app; imported data is checked more strictly
- Turning on a backup password now replaces older unencrypted backups on the phone and in Google Drive
- Calendar: a missed day can be logged or skipped from the calendar again
- Vial stock stays right after editing a compound or logging straight after starting a new vial
